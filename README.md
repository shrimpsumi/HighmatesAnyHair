# HighmatesAnyHair

lets highmates from vanilla races expanded put on any hairstyle without getting a look change desired or mood debuff.

## requirements

- [vanilla races expanded - highmate](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2995385834)
- ideology isn't required; without it, pawns don't get "look change desired" moods!

> [!NOTE]
>
> new highmate generations won't always spawn with highmate-specific hair since this mod removes the restriction from the 'beautiful-haired gene,' and equipping highmate hair requires said gene. small thing but bleghhh :3
